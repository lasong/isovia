## 2. General information on data processing

We process personal data of our users only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and
our content and services. Processing takes place on the basis of consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), for the
performance of contractual or pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or to safeguard legitimate
interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 3. Provision of the website and creation of log files

Each time our website is accessed, our system automatically collects data and information from the
accessing computer system (including IP address, date and time of access, browser and operating system
used, and page requested). This data is stored in log files and serves to ensure smooth operation and the
IT security of our systems (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This data is not combined with other data sources.

## 4. Hosting and database

This website is operated on **Salesforce, Inc. (Heroku)**, 415 Mission Street, San Francisco, CA 94105,
USA, in the **Europe region (Ireland data centre)**. Personal data collected through this website — in
particular the details submitted through the contact form — is stored on servers in Ireland and in a
PostgreSQL database (Heroku Postgres) operated there.

The hosting provider acts as a processor on our behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to
Art. 28 GDPR. Salesforce is a US company. According to the provider, certain technical operations — in
particular database backups and system and application logs — may also be processed in the USA. Where
such a transfer to a third country takes place, it is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses
(Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) together with supplementary safeguards. The legal basis for its use is our
legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 6. Contact and transport enquiries

If you contact us via the contact form, by email or by telephone, your details — in particular company,
name, position, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery location, radiopharmaceutical or
material, transport frequency, temperature requirement, type of enquiry and any further information you
provide — will be stored for the purpose of processing your enquiry and any follow-up questions.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as the enquiry serves to initiate a contract, and otherwise
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in efficiently handling enquiries addressed to us.

The mandatory fields are required so that we can assess and answer your transport enquiry. Please do not
submit any patient-related data through this form.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

This website uses **no** cookies beyond those that are technically necessary. We use no analytics or
tracking services, no advertising networks and no social media plugins. For this reason no cookie banner
is required and no profiling takes place.

A technically necessary cookie (`NEXT_LOCALE`) may be set for the language selection. It stores only your
chosen language and permits no conclusions about you as a person.
